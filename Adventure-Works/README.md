# Adventure Works Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Excel dashboard developed to analyze Adventure Works sales performance and provide insights into revenue, profitability, customer activity, order trends, and product performance.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Excel Slicers
- Data Visualization

## Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify yearly, quarterly, and monthly sales trends.
- Evaluate sales performance across countries and regions.
- Understand daily sales patterns.
- Compare monthly product costs against total sales.
- Monitor customer, order, quantity, and product performance.

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Sales: 29.36M
- Total Customers: 18,484
- Total Orders: 27,659
- Total Profit: 12.08M
- Total Quantity: 60,398
- Total Products: 130

## Key Insights

### 1. Overall Sales Performance
- Total sales reached approximately 29.36M.
- Total profit stood at approximately 12.08M.
- The business recorded 27,659 orders.
- A total of 18,484 customers contributed to sales.
- Total quantity sold was 60,398 across 130 products.

### 2. Year-wise Sales Performance
- 2011 recorded approximately 7.08M in sales.
- 2012 generated approximately 5.84M.
- 2013 recorded approximately 16.35M.
- 2013 was the highest among the displayed years.

### 3. Monthly Sales Trends
- December recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately 3.21M.
- October generated approximately 2.92M.
- November recorded approximately 2.98M.
- February recorded the lowest monthly sales at approximately 1.74M.

### 4. Quarterly Sales Performance
- Q4 recorded the highest quarterly sales at approximately 9.11M.
- Q3 contributed approximately 7.64M.
- Q2 generated approximately 7.09M.
- Q1 recorded approximately 5.52M.

### 5. Day-wise Sales Performance
- Tuesday recorded the highest daily sales at approximately 4.34M.
- Monday followed with approximately 4.24M.
- Wednesday generated approximately 4.23M.
- Thursday and Friday each recorded approximately 4.15M.
- Saturday recorded approximately 4.13M.
- Sunday generated approximately 4.11M.

### 6. Product Cost vs Sales Analysis
- Compares monthly sales amounts against total product costs.
- Helps understand monthly cost and sales movements.
- Supports monitoring of product cost trends.

### 7. Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing periods, particularly Q4.
- Investigate lower sales during Q1.
- Analyze regional sales performance.
- Monitor product costs against sales.
- Use daily sales trends for operational planning.
- Evaluate product category performance to identify growth opportunities.
